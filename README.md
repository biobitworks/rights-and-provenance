### Evidence custody and sealing

For released research and media, maintain source identity, author/rights class, original-byte SHA-256, version, transform lineage, accession or upstream reference, permission/consent state, receipt parent, and inclusion proof or append-only Merkle/MMR breakpoint **when actually computed**. Record denials, unknowns, and corrected predecessors without inventing positive results or replacing immutable source roots.

- A Git commit hash or Merkle root is an **integrity commitment**, **not** an authorization or copyright grant.
- `NOT_SIGNED` is different from a verifiable signature.
- `NOT_ANCHORED` is different from an independent timestamp or third-party witness.
- Signed provenance must identify an authorized signing key and support independent signature verification.
- Do not expose personal, private, regulated, restricted, or proprietary records merely to prove custody.
